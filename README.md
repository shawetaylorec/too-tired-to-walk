# Too Tired To Walk

A study timer, played as Kanto. Focused minutes buy walking distance; standing
in tall grass while you work is how you meet things.

**Play:** https://shawetaylorec.github.io/too-tired-to-walk/

It is one HTML file. Open it on a phone and add it to your home screen — it
installs as an app, goes full screen and keeps your save in the browser. There
is no account, no tracking, and nothing leaves your device.

`sandbox/` is the same game with every HM already in the bag, no tiredness and
the clock at ten times speed. It saves under a different key, so poking at it
cannot touch a real run.
